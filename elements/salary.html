Calculation Rules:
• Overtime = 125% of hourly rate
• Night Differential = additional 10% per hour
• Regular Holiday = 200% pay
• Special Holiday = 130% pay

Important Notes:
• This computation shows estimated NET salary before government deductions.
• Government deductions such as SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax, loans, and other company deductions are NOT included and are applied separately.

Holiday Computation Reminder:
• Holiday hours must be separated from regular hours for correct computation.
• Do NOT include holiday hours inside total regular hours.
• Example: If total worked hours = 104 and 8 hours are Regular Holiday, then:
  → Regular Hours = 96
  → Regular Holiday Hours = 8
• Adding holiday hours again to regular hours will result in double counting and incorrect pay.
